Multi-Layer Governance

The TRUST Protocol Verification System

Protecting landlords, tenants, and properties from fraud. Every listing and every user passes through a secure, step‑by‑step process designed to ensure truth, safety, and accountability.

1. Identity Verification

NIN-linked database matching and biometric face verification for both property owners and tenants.

2. Property Verification

Boots-on-the-ground physical inspections, title searches, and condition audits by TRUST certified agents.

3. Contract & Escrow

Legally-binding digital tenancy agreements, tamper-proof audit trails, and secure escrow accounts.

Layer 01

Identity Verification Gate

Before anyone can list or book a property, their legal identity is securely matched against the national database.

For Landlords

  • National ID registry matching (NIN linkage).
  • Face match (biometric selfie) validates the ID belongs to the actual user in real-time.
  • Prevents criminals from listing properties they do not own or possess.
Why this matters It protects your property from being claimed or listed by impersonators.

For Tenants

  • National ID + live selfie verification.
  • Ensures the person reserving the property is real, verifiable, and traceable.
  • Drastically reduces the risk of bad-faith occupants and fraudulent booking requests.
Why this matters It protects landlords from anonymous, unverified, or bad-faith tenants.
Layer 02

Property & Vetting Vows

No property goes live without verified physical and legal checks. We confirm authenticity first.

Certified Agent Auditing

Every property listed on the TRUST Protocol undergoes an on-site physical inspection by a certified TRUST inspector to confirm:

  • Existence: Physical confirmation that the building exists.
  • Ownership: Title deed validation at the land registry.
  • Condition: Verification that amenities and parameters match listing specifications.
  • No Misrepresentation: Eliminating hidden issues, outdated photos, or fake location logs.

TRUST Verified Badge

Only listings that pass all checks display this badge, ensuring full confidence and eliminating disputes.

Layer 03

Contract & Compliance

Establishing transparent, legally-binding, and tamper-proof relationships before keys are handed over.

Pre-Move-In Compliance Pipeline

Before keys change hands, both parties complete our automated compliance checks to ensure full transparency and legal protection.

Agreement Generation

A digital tenancy contract matching local real estate laws is automatically created.

Electronic Signing

Both landlord and tenant sign the document securely directly on the platform.

Secure Storage

Signed contracts are encrypted and stored, creating a tamper-proof legal record.

Escrow Activation

Funds are secured in escrow, aligning payments directly with move-in validation.

Why this matters It ensures absolute transparency, prevents verbal misunderstandings, and creates a legally binding, tamper‑proof contract record.
