Legal Architecture

TRUST Protocol Contract Standards

We enforce uniform, tamper‑proof, digitally signed contracts to protect both landlords and tenants. Every agreement follows strict standards designed to eliminate disputes and ensure transparency.

The Five Standards

Uniform Tenancy Governance

A legally enforceable framework that binds all parties to strict, standardized contract conditions.

Standard 01

Standardized Digital Agreements

  • Standard Terms Every tenancy uses the same verified contract template, ensuring fairness and consistency across all listings.
  • Clear Rights & Responsibilities Both parties receive a clear breakdown of obligations, payment terms, maintenance duties, and notice periods.
  • No Hidden Clauses All terms are visible, plain‑language, and approved by TRUST Protocol’s legal framework.
Standard 02

Digital Signatures & Secure Storage

  • Digital Signing Landlords and tenants sign electronically using verified, NIN-linked identities.
  • Tamper‑Proof Records Once signed, the contract is locked cryptographically and cannot be altered by either party.
  • Permanent Access Both parties can download or view the contract anytime from their dashboard.
Standard 03

Verified Information Only

  • Verified Identities Contracts are only generated after both landlord and tenant complete identity verification.
  • Verified Property Details All property information in the contract is based on the physical vetting report.
  • Verified Payment Terms Rent amounts, deposit details, and payment schedules are confirmed before signing.
Standard 04

Escrow‑Integrated Terms

  • Escrow Protection The contract includes mandatory escrow clauses to ensure safe, fraud‑proof payments.
  • Move‑In Confirmation Payment is only released after the tenant confirms successful move‑in.
  • Deposit Handling The security deposit remains in escrow until the end of the tenancy, with clear release conditions.
Standard 05

End‑of‑Tenancy Standards

  • Inspection Requirements A final inspection determines the condition of the property before deposit release.
  • Fair Damage Assessment Only documented, physical, and verifiable damage can be deducted from the deposit.
  • Dispute Resolution TRUST Protocol mediates using verified records, inspection reports, and escrow data.
