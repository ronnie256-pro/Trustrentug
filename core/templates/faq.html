General Questions

TRUST Protocol is a verification‑driven rental platform that protects tenants and landlords through identity checks, property vetting, and secure escrow payments.
It eliminates fake landlords, scam listings, cash‑loss risks, and undocumented agreements by ensuring every step is verified and transparent.

Verification

Landlords are verified using National ID + face match + ownership confirmation. This prevents impersonation and protects property owners from fraud.
Tenants complete National ID + selfie verification to ensure bookings come from real, traceable individuals.
Mandatory verification protects both sides from fraud, fake identities, and disputes.

Property Vetting

A TRUST‑certified inspector visits the property to confirm ownership, condition, accuracy of photos, and listing truthfulness.
Vetting prevents fake listings and ensures tenants only see verified, real homes.
Typically 24–72 hours depending on location and agent availability.

Escrow & Payments

Tenants pay the required rent period (1, 3, 6, or 12 months) plus deposit into escrow. Funds are released to the landlord only after verification and confirmed move‑in.
Escrow protects tenants from fake landlords and protects landlords from tenants who disappear after booking.
