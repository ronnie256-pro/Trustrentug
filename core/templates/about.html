Why we started TRUST

TRUST Protocol was created from a real experience which revealed how vulnerable families can be in Uganda’s property market. After relocating from Mozambique to Uganda, I set out to find a home for my family. A listing on a popular platform looked genuine. The agent seemed trustworthy. The house was real. The landlord appeared legitimate. The agreement was made, payment was sent and then both the agent and the landlord disappeared. The money was gone. The home was never mine to rent and there was no system in place to protect my family.
