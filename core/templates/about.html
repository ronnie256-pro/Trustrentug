The Founder's Story

TRUST Protocol was created from a real experience — one that revealed how vulnerable families can be in Uganda’s property market. After relocating from Mozambique to Uganda, I set out to find a home for my family. A listing on a popular platform looked genuine. The agent seemed trustworthy. The house was real. The landlord appeared legitimate. The agreement was made, payment was sent and then both the agent and the landlord disappeared. The money was gone. The home was never mine to rent and there was no system in place to protect my family.

Too many people lose money, time, and peace because the property market lacks verification, transparency, and secure payment processes.

TRUST Protocol was built to change that.

The Five Pillars of TRUST

T

Title Verification

Ownership documents strictly verified against central land registries.

R

Registered Landlord

Identity-linked dynamic accountability systems protecting tenants.

U

Urban Inspection

On-site physical validation of actual property conditions.

S

Secure Escrow

Rigorous financial protection framework ensuring safe transactions.

T

Transparent Contract

Legally-binding, fully encrypted digital agreements.

Our mission is simple:

To make property transactions safe, honest, and predictable for everyone.

We are a verification‑driven property platform designed to protect buyers, sellers, tenants, and diaspora clients. Every listing is verified. Every seller is authenticated. Every document is checked. Payments are held securely and released only when agreed conditions are met. Digital contracts ensure clarity and accountability. And for clients abroad, TRUST Protocol supervises construction projects, verifies land, and provides transparent progress updates.

TRUST Protocol exists so that no family whether in Uganda or abroad ever has to experience what I went through. We are building a future where trust is not assumed, but guaranteed.
