How Escrow Works on TRUST Protocol

Escrow is the safety engine of TRUST Protocol. It protects both tenants and landlords by ensuring that money is only released when the property, the landlord, and the move‑in are fully verified.

The Verified Process

Step-by-Step Security Pipeline

A rigorous, automated, and legally-binding mechanism that eliminates transactional risk for landlords and tenants.

1
Phase 01

Tenant Pays Into Escrow

The tenant pays the required rent period — whether 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or a full year — plus the security deposit into a secure escrow account.

No cash is handed over. No direct mobile money transfers. No risk of paying a fake landlord.

2
Phase 02

Property Is Physically Vetted

A TRUST‑certified inspector visits the property to physically inspect its parameters and verify details.

  • Confirm the house exists
  • Verify the landlord is the real owner
  • Audit condition against listing
  • Ensure no hidden issues or misrepresentations
3
Phase 03

Landlord Identity Is Verified

Before any listing goes live, the landlord's credentials and identity details are matched and validated against the national database.

This strictly prevents criminals from listing properties they do not own.

4
Phase 04

Tenant Confirms Move‑In

Once the inspection reports are approved, keys are handed over, and the tenant successfully moves in, they tap "Confirm Move‑In" on the platform.

This explicit digital confirmation triggers the final automated steps in the financial pipeline.

5
Phase 05

Escrow Releases Payment

Upon move-in confirmation:

  • Rent is released to the landlord
  • The security deposit remains safely held in escrow until the end of the tenancy
  • Both parties receive digital receipts and a verified contract
