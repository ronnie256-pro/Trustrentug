TRUST Protocol Dispute Resolution

TRUST Protocol provides a fair, transparent, evidence‑based dispute resolution system designed to protect both tenants and landlords. Eliminating guesswork and preventing exploitation.

Resolution Pipeline

Evidence‑Driven Mediation

Decisions structured strictly on verified platform logs, physical inspection records, and secure escrow status.

Standard 01

Evidence‑Based Resolution

  • Verified Records All decisions rely on verified identities, vetted property details, and time‑stamped platform activity.
  • Inspection Reports Move‑in and move‑out physical audits by certified inspectors provide objective proof of property condition.
  • Escrow Data Payment history, deposit status, and release conditions are fully documented.
Why this matters No one can lie, manipulate evidence, or alter records.
Standard 02

Transparent, Step‑by‑Step Process

  • Issue Submission Either party can instantly open a formal dispute ticket directly from their dashboard.
  • Review & Investigation TRUST Protocol reviews all verified data, inspection reports, and communication logs.
  • Fair Decision A resolution is issued based on facts — not emotion, pressure, or negotiation power.
Standard 03

Deposit Protection

  • Secure Deposit Handling The security deposit remains locked in escrow until the dispute is resolved.
  • Damage Assessment Only documented, verifiable physical damage can be deducted from deposit holdings.
  • Fair Refunds Tenants receive immediate refunds for any amount not tied to proven, inspector-verified damage.
Standard 04

Communication & Mediation

  • In‑App Communication All communication is securely recorded and time‑stamped for transparency.
  • Neutral Mediation TRUST Protocol acts as a neutral third party, ensuring neither side is disadvantaged.
  • Clear Outcomes Both parties receive a written resolution outlining responsibilities and next steps.
Standard 05

When Escalation Is Needed

  • Legal‑Ready Documentation If a dispute requires external legal action, TRUST Protocol provides verified records, inspection reports, and contract copies.
  • Tamper‑Proof Evidence All documents are digitally sealed and admissible as supporting legal evidence.
